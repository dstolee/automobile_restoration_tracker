# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, constructive, patient, and welcoming. Focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Accept responsibility and apologize when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publication of private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is inappropriate. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This code of conduct is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
